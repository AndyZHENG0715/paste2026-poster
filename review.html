Six models independently critiqued the poster (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, Gemini-3-Flash, Qwen3-VL, DeepSeek-vision, GLM-4.6V). Below is the deduplicated, prioritized consensus. Full per-model feedback is at the bottom.

Priority 1 — Theme & impact (the competition's core)

1. Fill the [X]% / [M] placeholders with real or clearly "promised" data. Placeholders read as unfinished and are the #1 judge red-flag.deepseek · gemini-3 · GPT-4o
2. Make the three “Trans-” pillars visually prominent. Add icon/colour badges or a triad near the title (Transdisciplinary · Transectoral · Transcultural) instead of burying them in small text tags.gemini-3 · qwen-vl · glm · deepseek · GPT-4o

Priority 2 — Visual impact & clarity

3. Enlarge the AI “digital twin” simulation graphic and add labelled callouts (water volume · pressure · nozzle) — it's the project's unique innovation but is too small to read.deepseek · gemini-3
4. Add a secondary accent colour (orange or teal) for key terms/highlights — the all-blue palette is safe but monotone.GPT-4o · qwen-vl · glm · gemini-3
5. Simplify the flowchart — fewer words per box, cleaner linear flow.gemini-3 · glm
6. Reduce text density — bullets over long paragraphs, more whitespace between sections.qwen-vl · glm · GPT-4o-mini
7. Standardise typography — one sans-serif, clear heading hierarchy, body ≥24pt for A0 distance.glm · deepseek · GPT-4o

Priority 3 — Polish

8. Swap any soft/low-res photos for sharp action shots and add one-line captions.qwen-vl
9. Reconsider the QR code — models disagree (top-right for visibility vs bottom-right to declutter the footer).GPT-4o · qwen-vl
10. Trim references to ~5 and add a one-line “Conclusion / Future work”.deepseek · GPT-4o-mini
